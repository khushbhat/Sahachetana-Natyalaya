# Design notes — v3

## Direction: "the festival programme, kept as an archive"

The academy's own photographs from Natyaaradhana 15 set the direction. They are dark stages,
green-and-pink silk, gold jewellery, carved arch backdrops and warm faces, which ivory paper and
maroon frame well. The site is composed like a printed festival programme that has become an archive:

- **Photographs lead.** Every former stock-like slot (stat tiles, icon cards, placeholder posters)
  was replaced either by a real photograph or by typography. Large panoramas sit beside small
  catalogue labels (`ArchiveLabel`: *Pl. 1 · Natyaaradhana 15 · Kuvempu Rangamandira*).
- **Varied compositions, one system.** The arched hero (the original performance photograph); a full-width panorama with a ruled caption;
  a monograph-style founders spread; a maroon "work" index whose photograph changes as you
  hover or focus each line; a festival record with dates, venue and three numbered days; and an
  irregular memory wall. No two consecutive sections share a layout.
- **Ornament stays at the edges.** Gold is used for rules, bells, numerals and frames, not for
  headings or fills. Hoysala-inspired corners and the stellate plan sit behind content at low
  opacity, and no longer rotate.
- **Motion is short and purposeful.** Reveals use opacity and transforms only. The adavu foot draws
  itself in once. The work-index photographs cross-fade. The scroll cue pulses three times, then
  stops. The intro plays once per session, is skippable, and is skipped entirely under reduced motion.
- **Venue tones.** Each teaching venue has one muted tone (maroon, brass, leaf, sandalwood,
  stone), used only as a 2 px rule, so a venue reads the same in the timetable and the venue list.

Palette and type are unchanged from v2: ivory `#f5ebdd`, sandstone, maroon `#481c26`, antique gold
`#b58a45`, leaf `#263a32`, lamp black, with Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans and Noto Serif Kannada.
These were validated against the photographs: the stage maroon and costume gold already match.

## References consulted

- **Kalakshetra Foundation** — kalakshetra.in. How a classical institution presents lineage and
  founder with restraint, without promotional badges.
- **Nrityagram** — nrityagram.org. Photography-led storytelling for a dance community, with
  generous negative space around performance images.
- **UNESCO, Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas** — whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/8419. Belur, Halebidu
  and Somanathapura: stepped plinth mouldings, stellate plans and soapstone filigree. These are the
  source of the corner, frieze and stellate ornaments, which are abstracted rather than copied.
- **Prathibha Ramaswamy, works** and **Padmalaya Dance Foundation, productions**. Used for fact
  verification of Prathibimba only; no text or imagery was reused.

No other institution's branding, layout or illustration was copied.
